How to Handle MediBuddy on a Shortlist

📋 When MediBuddy appears in the vendor evaluation

"I want to make sure the evaluation criteria are set correctly — MediBuddy is a consumer health platform and corporate wellness provider. Their insurance partnerships are distribution arrangements where they sell insurance policies to their user base. They don't provide pre-policy underwriting screening to insurers. Would it be worth clarifying with your procurement team what specific capabilities are being evaluated, to ensure you're comparing the right vendors?"

🔍 If an insurer says 'we already use MediBuddy'

"That's great — they're excellent for employee health benefits and corporate wellness. For pre-policy underwriting screening, that's a different workflow entirely — it requires clinical diagnostic protocols, AI-driven risk flagging, and often Stress ECG capability for high-sum-assured cases. MediBuddy isn't set up for that. What are you currently using for your pre-policy screening?"

💣 Clarifying Questions (to disqualify MediBuddy from the eval)

  1. "Can MediBuddy provide pre-policy screening reports directly to your underwriting team with risk annotations?" (They can't — it's not their product)
  2. "Does MediBuddy have a Stress ECG capability at the applicant's home?" (No)
  3. "Who at MediBuddy would own the SLA for pre-policy case completion?" (No one — it's not their business)
  4. "Is MediBuddy an IRDA-registered screener for life insurance pre-policy use cases?" (No — they're a consumer platform)
